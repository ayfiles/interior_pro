REALISM: Real camera footage in physical space, indistinguishable from reality.

CAMERA: Full-frame, 35mm or 50mm. Natural subtle depth of field (no fake blur). Correct exposure, realistic dynamic range (no HDR).

IMPERFECTIONS: Slight noise, subtle lens distortion, micro shadows.

LIGHTING & SHADOWS: Completely natural, physically accurate. Soft daylight from outside + warm ambient interior lighting. Real light interaction: sunlight reflecting exactly as reality, correct reflections, soft highlights, natural diffusion, accurate shadow falloff, subtle light transitions, realistic contrast. No artificial light effects.

ATMOSPHERE: Warm, real, physically believable. In visible sun rays, subtle airborne dust particles present (clearly visible, natural, not exaggerated).

Highest frame rate for ultra-fluid motion. Feel like high-end stabilized gimbal (continuous, zero jitter/stepping/micro-shakes).

COLOR/GRADING: Neutral natural tones, slight warmth, realistic white balance. No stylized grading/LUT.

AVOID: CGI/render look, overly clean edges, perfect geometry, flickering, warping, AI artifacts, over-sharpening, fake details.

OUTPUT: 4K, ultra smooth motion, high-end architectural real-location footage.
