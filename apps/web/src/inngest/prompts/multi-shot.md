# Kling Multi-Shot Prompt

Create a multi shot ultra-photorealistic video of a modern interior, based exactly on the provided image. The result must look like real camera footage in a real space - not rendered, not AI-generated.

CRITICAL: nothing may be changed, added, or removed; all elements, layout, proportions, furniture, and styling must stay 100% identical; no hallucinations.

STRUCTURE: For a 10 second multi-shot clip, create exactly five elegant shot changes / perspectives, each about 2 seconds long. The sequence should feel like: 1) wide establishing room view, 2) close-up of the main object or furniture detail, 3) alternate room angle, 4) material/atmosphere detail shot, 5) final premium hero perspective. Each change should feel intentional, smooth, cinematic, and rhythmically connected.

Absolute photorealism with slight noise, subtle lens distortion, micro shadows, full-frame camera look (35mm or 50mm), very subtle natural depth of field, correct exposure, realistic dynamic range (no HDR look).

Lighting and shadows must be physically accurate; sunlight interacts with materials realistically, creating correct reflections, highlights, diffusion, and natural light bounce for depth and contrast. Sunlight entering the camera must behave like a real lens: realistic glare, subtle natural lens flare, correct exposure response, slight highlight bloom - no artificial effects.

Soft daylight combined with warm interior lighting, realistic shadow softness and falloff.

Atmosphere: airborne dust particles visible in sunlight rays must look maximally realistic, physically correct in size, density, and motion - subtle but natural, no exaggeration.

Materials: maximum textile detail with visible fibers, realistic wood grain, slight stone imperfections, accurate metal reflections; no plastic look or overly perfect surfaces.

Environment: curtains must move with maximally realistic, physically accurate motion, natural wind interaction, organic irregular movement, no looping or artificial patterns.

Background (if visible): water movement must be highly realistic with natural waves and reflections; birds fly naturally and subtly.

Camera: five cinematic shots focusing on the room, material details, alternate angles, atmosphere, and a final hero view. Each shot should use slow, smooth stabilized movement, perfectly stable, no jitter, highest FPS, high-end gimbal feel; highlight texture, light interaction, and micro details.

Color: neutral, slightly warm, realistic white balance, no stylized grading.

Avoid CGI look, perfect geometry, artifacts, oversharpening.

Final: ultra smooth high-end architectural video, indistinguishable from real footage.
