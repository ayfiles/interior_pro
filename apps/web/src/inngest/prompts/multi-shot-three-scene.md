# Kling Multi-Shot Three-Scene Prompt

Create a 10 second multi-shot ultra-photorealistic video of a modern interior, based exactly on the provided image. The result must look like real camera footage in a real space - not rendered, not AI-generated.

CRITICAL: nothing may be changed, added, or removed; all elements, layout, proportions, furniture, switched-on lights, materials, and styling must stay 100% identical; no hallucinations.

STRUCTURE: Create exactly three elegant shot changes / perspectives across the 10 second clip. The sequence should feel like: 1) premium alternate establishing angle, 2) refined close-up on the strongest object or material detail, 3) final architectural hero perspective. The pacing should be slower and more luxurious than the five-shot version. Do not collapse the request into one long camera move.

Absolute photorealism with slight noise, subtle lens distortion, micro shadows, full-frame camera look (35mm or 50mm), very subtle natural depth of field, correct exposure, realistic dynamic range (no HDR look).

Lighting and shadows must be physically accurate. Preserve the original color identity of all objects and materials while allowing cinematic daylight, warm practical glow, natural contrast, realistic highlights, and soft shadow falloff.

Atmosphere: subtle airborne dust particles in visible sun rays, natural curtain movement if present, realistic reflections in metal, glass, wood, stone, and fabric.

Camera: three cinematic shots only, each smooth, stable, and intentional. Use slow gimbal movement, alternate perspectives, and elegant detail framing. No bounce, no vertical bobbing, no jitter, no artificial motion, no rushed montage feeling.

Color: neutral, slightly warm, realistic white balance, premium architectural campaign feeling.

Avoid CGI look, perfect geometry, artifacts, oversharpening, fake details, color drift, or changed furniture.

Final: ultra smooth high-end architectural video, indistinguishable from real footage.
